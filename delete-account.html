Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: Kivaloop Shop Partner  |  Package: com.mobile.kivaloopShop

Important Notice: Deleting your Kivaloop Shop Partner account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your business profile data, menu items, images, and partner records will be wiped from our active databases.

How to Delete Your Account

As per Google Play Developer Policy, we provide two convenient ways to request full deletion of your account and associated personal data:

Method 1: Directly Inside the Mobile App (Instant)

If you currently have the app installed on your phone:

  1. Open the Kivaloop Shop Partner app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the profile screen.
  5. Tap the Delete Account button.
  6. Confirm the action in the prompt by tapping Delete Permanently.

Your account, shop documents, and menu catalog are deleted immediately upon confirmation.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Without Installing the App)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion by submitting your registered account details below:

Request Submitted! An email deletion ticket has been initiated. If your mail client did not open automatically, please send an email to support@kivaloop.com with subject "Account Deletion Request".

Data Types That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your email address, encrypted authentication records, and user UID in Google Firebase.
  • Shop Profile & Business Details: Store name, description, address, city, ZIP code, operating hours, and weekly schedule in Cloud Firestore.
  • Location Data: Geolocation coordinates associated with your store.
  • Menu & Inventory: All item names, descriptions, categories, prices, and options.
  • Media & Images: Store logo, banner, and item pictures hosted on Cloudinary.

Data Retention Policy

Operational data: Deleted immediately upon in-app action or within 7 business days upon receiving a web request.
Legal & Financial records: Any completed historical payment or invoice receipts required by local taxation, accounting, or anti-fraud laws may be securely archived in anonymized form for the legally mandated period.
