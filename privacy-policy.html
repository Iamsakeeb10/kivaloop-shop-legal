Privacy Policy

App Name: Kivaloop Shop Partner  |  Package: com.mobile.kivaloopShop  |  Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Overview: This Privacy Policy describes how Kivaloop ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal and business information when you use the Kivaloop Shop Partner mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you register as a merchant partner or operate your store through the Kivaloop Shop Partner app, we collect the following types of information:

A. Account & Owner Information

  • Full Name: To identify the primary account holder and store representative.
  • Email Address: Used for account authentication, password recovery, and important service notifications.
  • Phone Number: Used for merchant verification and customer order coordination.

B. Store & Business Profile Information

  • Shop Name & Description: Displayed to customers searching for food, beverages, and retail items.
  • Store Address & City: To establish store presence and compute delivery/pickup zones.
  • Business Operating Hours & Weekly Schedule: To let customers know when your shop is open or closed.
  • Menu & Inventory Items: Item names, descriptions, prices, categories, and availability.

C. Location Information

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS): With your permission, we collect device location coordinates when you register or update your shop address to place your store accurately on customer maps and calculate distance for nearby customer discovery. We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed.

D. Photos & Media

  • Shop Logo & Banner Images: Uploaded by you to showcase your storefront.
  • Menu Item Photos: Uploaded to illustrate menu selections.
  • Images are uploaded securely to our trusted cloud media provider (Cloudinary).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • To create and manage your Shop Partner account and authenticate logins.
  • To list your shop and menu items on customer-facing Kivaloop platforms.
  • To manage and deliver incoming customer orders in real time.
  • To provide business metrics, revenue summaries, and order history on your partner dashboard.
  • To provide customer support and notify you of critical app updates or policy changes.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and ensure compliance with our merchant guidelines.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We work with trusted third-party service providers that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Authentication) and real-time cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore). Privacy details: Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary: Used as a secure cloud media storage and content delivery network (CDN) for uploaded shop and menu photos. Privacy details: Cloudinary Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal and business information only as long as your partner account remains active. You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app by navigating to Profile → Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, you can submit an account and data deletion request through our Account Deletion Page.

Upon receiving a deletion request, your authentication credentials, shop profile, menu items, and media files will be permanently deleted from our active databases.

5. Security of Your Data

We take appropriate administrative, technical, and physical measures to safeguard your personal data. All communications between the mobile app, Firebase, and Cloudinary are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Authentication credentials are encrypted and managed through Google Firebase secure infrastructure.

6. Children's Privacy

Kivaloop Shop Partner is intended exclusively for business owners, merchant partners, and individuals of legal business age (at least 18 years old). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
